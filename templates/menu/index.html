How AI Continuously Learns Old & New Dishes

When you add a brand-new dish (such as Dosa and Curry), it is saved immediately to the menu and database. Every single customer order placed for any dish (old or new) is recorded into the historical sales database and triggers an automatic AI model retrain — so future predictions on the Forecasting Dashboard always reflect both older dishes and newly introduced dishes. If a dish underperforms or isn't profitable, delete it anytime here.
